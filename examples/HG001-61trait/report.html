Both hypo- and hyperthyroidism score elevated here — that's expected, not contradictory. Graves' (over-active) and Hashimoto's (under-active) are both autoimmune thyroid disease and share risk genes, so a high score for one often means a high score for the other. One person can have both across a lifetime: Hashimoto's can begin with a brief over-active phase, and treated Graves' frequently ends up under-active.

How to read every row

Icon
The body system the trait affects (brain, heart, thyroid, …) — a visual anchor, not a result.
Likelihood bar
Where your score sits vs others of the same ancestry (left = lower, right = higher). The tick is the population average.
Confidence
How much to trust the number. High = large, replicated, ancestry-checked studies. Insufficient = thin science; we don't translate it into a risk.
Lifetime chance
The percentile turned into an actual probability using the disease's effect size and how common it is — shown only when confidence allows.
Coverage
How much of the score we could measure in your data. Completeness, not quality.
