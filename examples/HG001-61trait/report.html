How to read every card

Percentile
Your rank vs others of the same ancestry — not your chance of the disease. "Top 1%" = higher score than 99 of 100 people.
Confidence
How much to trust the number. High = large, replicated, ancestry-checked studies. Insufficient = thin science; we don't translate it into a risk.
Lifetime chance
The percentile turned into an actual probability using the disease's effect size and how common it is — shown only when confidence allows.
Coverage
How much of the score we could measure in your data. Completeness, not quality.
